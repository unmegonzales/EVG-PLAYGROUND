Install in Reserve

  1. Open Settings → Edit User → Email Signature.
  2. Select the HTML tab, then open source view (<>).
  3. Paste the contents of paste-into-reserve.html.
  4. Replace the phone and email with your live contact details.
  5. Keep Include Signature in New Emails checked, then save.
